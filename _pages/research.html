Research Projects

  • Developing an In-Service Training Framework for Faculty Members in English-Medium Universities (TÜBİTAK 1001, Project No. 121K227)
    Researcher (Scholarship Holder) · 2021–2025
    A TÜBİTAK-funded project focused on developing a data-driven in-service training framework for faculty members in English-medium universities. My contributions included collecting and analysing approximately 150 hours of multimodal EMI classroom interaction data, preparing detailed transcriptions, developing and administering surveys, conducting qualitative and quantitative analyses, and contributing to the development of the EMIT4F training programme and MOOC modules. 🔗
